Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Asiye and Asiye Group collect, use, disclose, protect, retain and delete personal information when you use the Asiye passenger app, driver app, websites and related transport, Asiye Work, delivery, wallet, safety and support services.

1. Scope and who we are

This policy applies to Asiye services operated under the Asiye name, including passenger and driver applications, Asiye Work shared commuting, delivery services, account and profile features, support, notifications, trip communications and wallet functions.

References to “Asiye”, “we”, “us” or “our” mean Asiye and Asiye Group as the developer and operator of these services. References to “you” mean a passenger, driver, applicant, website visitor or other user of an Asiye service.

2. Information we collect

The information we collect depends on which Asiye services you use. It may include the following categories.

Account and contact information

Name, phone number, email address, account identifiers, sign-in provider and account type.

Profile and identity information

Profile image, live face-scan final image, and information used to verify passenger or driver profiles.

Driver verification information

Driver licence or permit images, identity documents, vehicle photos and details, references, verification status and related application information.

Location and route information

Precise or approximate location, pickup and destination coordinates, routes, driver live location, and saved Home or Work places.

Trip and delivery information

Ride requests, Asiye Work pools, pickup PINs, passenger groups, trip status, recipient details, parcel information, fares, cancellations and trip history.

Communications and support

Trip chat messages, support requests, admin notes, feedback and communications with Asiye.

Selected contacts

When you choose a contact using the device contact picker, Asiye receives only the contact details you select for the relevant feature. We do not need to copy your entire address book.

Device and app information

Notification token, app version, operating-system information, diagnostics, notification interaction and technical information used for security and service operation.

Wallet and transaction information

Wallet balance, top-up amount, payment status, provider transaction references, refunds or adjustments and related transaction records.

Driver banking and payout information

Account holder, bank, account number, branch code, account type and payout or withdrawal information supplied by drivers.

Live face scan

Asiye uses the front camera to guide a user through a live face scan. The app may ask the user to centre their face, move and smile. The final captured image can be saved as the profile or verification image. The movement sequence itself is not intended to be stored as a video.

3. Device permissions and sensitive access

Depending on your device and the feature you use, Asiye may request:

  • Location for pickup detection, route calculation, nearby-driver matching, driver navigation, trip progress and safety features.
  • Camera for live face scanning, profile images, driver verification and document or vehicle capture.
  • Microphone where a camera or media feature requires audio permission. Asiye does not use microphone access to continuously record ordinary trips.
  • Notifications for booking requests, driver acceptance, trip progress, account updates, safety alerts and service messages.
  • Contact picker when you actively choose a contact for a safety, referral or related feature.
  • Files or media on supported devices where you choose a document or image to upload. Older Android versions may expose legacy storage permissions.

You can control device permissions in your phone settings. Some Asiye functionality may not work if a permission required for that feature is disabled.

4. How we use information

  • Create, authenticate and maintain passenger and driver accounts.
  • Verify profiles, driver applications, vehicles and required documents.
  • Match passengers, shared Asiye Work groups and deliveries with drivers.
  • Calculate routes, prices, estimated times and pickup or destination information.
  • Enable trip communications, safety features, notifications and customer support.
  • Process wallet top-ups, payment confirmations, driver payouts and financial records.
  • Detect abuse, investigate fraud, enforce platform rules and protect users and the service.
  • Improve reliability, troubleshoot errors, monitor service performance and develop features.
  • Meet legal, accounting, safety, regulatory or dispute-resolution obligations.

Asiye does not sell personal information as a business model. We use personal information for the purposes described in this policy and disclose it only as described below.

5. How information is shared

Passengers and drivers

Information necessary to perform a booking can be shared between the matched passenger and driver, such as name, profile image, pickup or destination information, trip status, vehicle information, rating and contact or messaging details needed to complete the service.

Technology and service providers

We use providers that process information on our behalf or provide infrastructure needed to operate Asiye. These may include:

  • Google Firebase for authentication, database, messaging, backend functions and related infrastructure.
  • Google Sign-In and Apple Sign-In where a user chooses those account methods.
  • Mapbox and, where enabled, Google Maps / Places services for address search, geocoding, mapping and route functions.
  • Ozow and PayFast for supported payment and wallet top-up flows.
  • Asiye-hosted servers, including app.asiye.cloud, for application content and image or document uploads.
  • Hosting, communications, diagnostics, security and professional service providers reasonably necessary to operate the platform.

Legal and safety disclosures

We may disclose information where required by law, legal process or a valid authority request, or where reasonably necessary to protect a person, investigate fraud, respond to a safety incident, enforce our rights or protect the Asiye platform.

6. Payments and financial information

Payment providers may collect payment or banking information directly from you under their own privacy terms. Asiye receives information needed to identify and reconcile a transaction, such as an amount, status, merchant reference or provider transaction identifier.

Asiye does not require users to provide online-banking passwords to Asiye. Drivers may provide bank-account details to Asiye for payout or verification purposes.

7. Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect personal information, including authenticated access, transport encryption where supported, server-side controls for sensitive admin actions, restricted backend credentials and platform access controls.

No internet or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their device, verification codes and account credentials and should report suspected unauthorised access.

8. Data retention and account deletion

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the Asiye service, maintain security, resolve disputes and meet legal, accounting, tax, fraud-prevention or safety obligations. Different categories of information may therefore have different retention periods.

When you request account deletion, Asiye will delete or de-identify personal information associated with the account where it is no longer required. Certain limited records may be retained where there is a legitimate legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention, accounting, safety or dispute-resolution reason to keep them. Access to retained information is limited to the purpose for which it is retained.

Request deletion without the app

You can start an account and data deletion request at https://asiye.cloud/delete/. You do not need to reinstall the Asiye app to use that page.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, including South African data-protection requirements where applicable, you may ask us to access, correct, update or delete personal information, object to certain processing, or raise a privacy concern.

You can also update certain account information inside Asiye, control device permissions in your phone settings, turn off notifications at device level, and request deletion through the deletion page linked above.

10. Children

Asiye is not designed as a child-directed service. Driver accounts require the qualifications and permissions applicable to lawful driving and transport services. If you believe personal information relating to a child has been provided to Asiye contrary to applicable law, contact us so that we can review the matter.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Asiye features, service providers, legal requirements or data practices change. We will post the updated version at this URL and change the effective date above. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or another appropriate channel.
