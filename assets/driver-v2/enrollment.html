Checking your application…

1 · Identity and vehicle scans

Use the camera to scan your selfie, vehicle and ID/passport. These scans stay in the application until you submit for verification.

2 · Driver’s licence scan

Place the whole licence inside the camera frame and make sure all details are readable.

3 · Personal and vehicle details

For example: 082 123 4567 or +27 82 123 4567. Spaces, brackets and hyphens are allowed.

4 · Three references

Provide three different people who have agreed to be contacted.

5 · Banking details

Use the details from your bank statement. Enter numbers only for your account number and six-digit branch code.

6 · Review and submit

Check your details before sending your application. Banking account numbers are masked below.

Your scans and details are securely submitted only when you tap Submit for verification.

Your entries stay while you move between steps. Closing or refreshing this page clears unsent details.
